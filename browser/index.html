Vemana in your browser

Vemana is a 278M-parameter Telugu language model trained from scratch. Here it runs entirely inside this tab: the 297 MB model downloads once and is kept by your browser, and your text never leaves your device. It is a base model: give it the start of some Telugu text and it writes on.

Works best on a laptop or a recent phone with a good connection.

Vemana continues text; it does not answer questions or follow instructions, and it makes things up. Sampling: temperature 0.8, top-p 0.95, up to 160 new tokens.
